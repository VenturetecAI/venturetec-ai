Provenance
Dataset: MSCI-aligned enabler benchmark, 18 building blocks across Culture, Capability and Capacity.
Methodology: peer scores normalised to a 1 to 5 scale; Median, Q1 (top quartile) and Q3 (bottom quartile) reported per block. Range bar shows the Q1 to Q3 band with a median marker.
Basis: aggregated peer assessments by industry and geography. Cells without peer coverage show a dash.
Use: directional peer context only, not a certification or audited financial figure.
